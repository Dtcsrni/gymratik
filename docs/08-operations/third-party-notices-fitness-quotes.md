# Atribuciones de frases y retrato

Las frases originales breves de Arnold Schwarzenegger incluidas en la rotación se basan en estas publicaciones oficiales. La versión en español es una traducción de Gymratik:

- “Don't wait until you feel like it. Just start. The action creates the feeling. Every rep builds it.” — [You Don’t Have to Feel Like It](https://www.schwarzenegger.com/fitness/post/you-dont-have-to-feel-like-it)
- “The reason is simple: hard work works. Consistency works.” — [Build a Foundation That No One Can Tear Down](https://www.schwarzenegger.com/fitness/post/build-a-foundation-that-no-one-can-tear-down)
- “Don't wait for motivation to find you; go find it today.” — [The Spark](https://www.schwarzenegger.com/index.php/fitness/post/the-spark)
